Search engine optimisation

SEO.

SEO that starts in the code. Structure, speed and substance, so search engines understand your site and people choose it.

How search works

Crawl. Index. Rank.

01

Crawl

Search engines discover pages by following links and sitemaps. If they can't reach a page, it can't rank.

02

Index

Content is analysed and stored. Clear structure, titles and structured data help engines understand what a page is about.

03

Rank

Pages are ordered by relevance, quality and experience: speed, mobile usability and trust all play a part.

Core Web Vitals

Speed you can measure.

0 s

Largest Contentful Paint

Good is 2.5 seconds or less. Poor is above 4 seconds. How fast the main content appears.

0 ms

Interaction to Next Paint

Good is 200 milliseconds or less. Poor is above 500 ms. How quickly the page responds to input.

0

Cumulative Layout Shift

Good is 0.1 or less. Poor is above 0.25. How stable the layout is while loading.

Four pillars

What good SEO covers.

01

Technical

Crawlability, indexing, HTTPS, sitemaps, canonical URLs, redirects, mobile usability and speed. The base everything else sits on.

02

On-page

Unique titles and descriptions, a logical heading structure, meaningful internal links, image alt text and clean URLs.

03

Content

Pages that answer real questions in plain language, matched to what people are actually searching for.

04

Authority & local

Reputation signals such as genuine reviews, a complete Google Business Profile, consistent business details and earned mentions.
