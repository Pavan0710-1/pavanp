    Web design in Sydney, explained plainly.

    If you are looking for a web designer in Sydney, you probably want three things: a site that looks right, loads fast and can be found by customers.

    04 — Principles

    How I think.

    01

    Speed is design

    A beautiful site that loads slowly is a bad site. Performance is part of the visual design, not final polish.

    02

    Clarity beats cleverness

    Visitors should know what you do and what to do next within seconds. Motion and 3D support that goal.

    03

    Built to be found

    Search engine optimisation

    SEO.

    SEO that starts in the code. Structure, speed and substance, so search engines understand your site and people choose it.

    How search works

    Crawl. Index. Rank.

    01

    Crawl

    Engines discover pages by following links and sitemaps. If they can't reach a page, it can't rank.

    02

    Index

    Content is analysed and stored. Clear structure, titles and structured data help engines understand each page.

    03

    Rank

    Pages are ordered by relevance, quality and experience. Speed, mobile usability and trust all play a part.

    Core Web Vitals

    Speed you can measure.

    0 s

    Largest Contentful Paint

    Good is 2.5 seconds or less. Poor is above 4 seconds.

    0 ms

    Interaction to Next Paint

    Good is 200 ms or less. Poor is above 500 ms.

    0

    Cumulative Layout Shift

    Good is 0.1 or less. Poor is above 0.25.

    Four pillars

    What good SEO covers.

    01

    Technical

    Crawlability, indexing, HTTPS, sitemaps, canonicals, redirects, mobile usability and speed. The base everything sits on.

    02

    On-page

    Unique titles and descriptions, one H1 per page, logical headings, meaningful internal links, alt text and clean URLs.

    03

    Content

    Pages that answer real questions in plain language, matched to what people actually search for.

    04

    Authority & local

    Genuine reviews, a complete Google Business Profile, consistent business details and earned mentions.

    Local

    Found in Sydney.

    If customers search with a suburb or "near me", local SEO matters.

    That means a complete Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details everywhere, genuinely useful location pages and a steady habit of asking happy customers for reviews.

    • Google Business Profile guidance
    • LocalBusiness structured data
    • Review and citation strategy, never bought
    AI search

    Ready for AI answers too.

    Search is changing. AI assistants now summarise answers, so clear, well-structured, factual content matters more than ever.

    • Plain-language answers to real questions, in headings and short paragraphs
    • Structured data (JSON-LD) that matches visible content
    • An llms.txt summary file for AI crawlers
    • Consistent business facts across your site and profiles
    • Fast, crawlable, semantic pages

      Packet Run · how to play

      Steer. Dodge. Ping.

      You are a data packet in a network that keeps getting faster. Stay connected as long as you can.

      • Steer: A / D or arrow keys, or move the mouse. On touch, drag your finger.
      • PING: press Space or Shift, click the mouse or tap the PING button to dash. You are invulnerable while dashing. It recharges in about 3 seconds.
      • Collect bytes: glowing gems add to your score.
      • Power-ups: blue Encrypt rings add a shield that absorbs a hit. Pink Overclock doubles your score for 8 seconds.
      • Obstacles: Firewalls have a gap to fly through, Servers are pillars to dodge and Scanners sweep side to side.
      • Pause: Esc or P. Turn the music on: it intensifies with your speed.
      Bug Storm · how to play

      Move. Survive. Evolve.

      Your ship fires automatically. You just have to stay alive and choose well.

      • Move: WASD or arrow keys. Hold the mouse to steer toward the cursor. On touch, use the joystick.
      • Collect: glowing gems dropped by bugs. Fill the bar to level up.
      • Choose: pick 1 of 3 upgrades each level (press 1, 2 or 3).
      • Pause: Esc or P.
      • Boss: a Memory Leak arrives every 90 seconds. Kill it for a huge reward.
      • Secrets: type "play" for Bug Storm or "run" for Packet Run on any page (outside text fields).
